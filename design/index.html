The loop

Diagnose, recommend, execute, measure.

1

Diagnose

Reads up to 10 pages of your site, or your channel's recent videos, and runs plain checks you can verify yourself.

14 readiness checks · Google PageSpeed · YouTube public data

2

Recommend

Ranks the changes by impact and effort. Every item points at the line of evidence it rests on.

Priority comes from a fixed rule, not a hidden score

3

Execute

Drafts titles, page copy, hooks and scripts from your own facts. You pick a version. You publish it.

Never publishes, buys or edits anything for you

4

Measure

Run it again and see what is fixed, what is still there, and what is new. No claim that a change caused a result.

Checks that pass or fail, not predictions

Why you can trust it

We quote it, or we don't say it.

Every finding is tagged with where it came from. A finding whose evidence cannot be found in your own pages or data is removed before you see it.

Observed

Measured from your public pages, PageSpeed or YouTube data.

You told us

Typed in by you. Used, but never presented as verified.

AI-derived

A judgement, worded as a likelihood. Never as a fact.

Unavailable

Data we cannot get, such as watch time. Shown struck through, never as zero.
