Your site loads fast, but visitors can't tell what you sell.

Bare Botanics sells plant-based skincare to adults with sensitive skin. Your website is technically sound, your domain suits the brand, and your YouTube channel is active. The biggest gap is the homepage: it doesn't say what you offer or who it's for, and that affects both search engines and AI tools.

Recommended next step

Say what you sell, and for whom, in one heading and one sentence on the homepage.

It is the one change that helps all three areas: search engines get a clear title and heading, AI tools get a plain statement of what the business is, and visitors know they are in the right place. It is low effort and the evidence is quoted on the SEO page.
