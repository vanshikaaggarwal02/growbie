Do you need a website?

AI-derived assessment
Yes.

You plan to sell classes by booking, so people need somewhere to see dates, prices and the location, and to book. Social profiles alone make that harder. A single page is enough to start.

The minimum digital foundation

  • A domain name that matches the businessOptional to buy now; see the ideas on the right
  • A one-page site: what you teach, who it's for, where you are, how to book
  • A Google Business Profile so you appear on mapsAdded when you have an address
  • A clear title, description and one main heading on the page
  • A contact and booking path that works on a phone

Not started items are shown as open, not failed.
