How a change recommendation would look (example for design review)
What is limiting
The domain is unrelated to the business name, so customers searching for the brand might not find it.
Why a change could help
A name that matches the brand is easier to remember and share.
Risks
Search rankings can dip during a move, old links break without redirects, and email must be moved.
Is it worth it?
Only if the brand mismatch is hurting you. Nothing should change until you've weighed the risks. We never buy or change anything for you.
